## Decision: Add a tenth category, "AI-Native CRM and Systems of Record", to the AI GTM Market Map from the Q1 2027 edition

## Context
Dreamteam (vendor #801) raised $53M on Nov 16 2026 for an AI-native CRM. Five days earlier, Rox raised a $100M Series B at $1.3B for revenue agents built on an agentic CRM layer. The nine-category map (Q4 2026, post 662) has no CRM category: Salesforce and HubSpot appear only as context, and Attio and Aurasell, which have profiles on the site, are not on the map at all. Dreamteam had nowhere to sit.

## Alternatives considered
1. **New Category 10: AI-Native CRM and Systems of Record.** Dreamteam, Rox, Attio and Aurasell are tracked; the incumbents are context. (Chosen.)
2. **Category 3 (AI SDR):** put Dreamteam beside Rox as an "agentic CRM" position. This is the lightest change, but Dreamteam isn't an SDR tool, and it would blur Category 3's scope.
3. **Watchlist only:** mention Dreamteam in carry-forward without placing it. This avoids a structural change, but it ignores a layer that has now drawn two sizeable rounds in a week.

## Reasoning
Every other category on the map writes into a CRM. When venture money goes into replacing the system of record itself, that is a distinct layer and it deserves its own column. Our coverage already supports it: Attio and Aurasell have profiles, there are CRM comparisons (attio-vs-hubspot, attio-vs-pipedrive, hubspot-vs-salesforce), and the site has a CRM vendor_category (45). Putting Dreamteam in Category 3 would mis-describe it.

## Trade-offs accepted
- The map is no longer "nine categories", so earlier editions and their predictions aren't strictly comparable for this layer.
- More research load each quarter.
- A theme change (a tenth column in inc/market-map.php and the graphic) that a human must apply.
- Rox currently sits under AI SDR on the tracker and may need to sit in both categories.

## Supersedes
None. This extends the nine-category structure used since the Q2 2026 edition.
